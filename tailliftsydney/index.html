Types of Tail Lifts

Oh boy! When it comes to the transport and logistics industry, tail lifts are a crucial piece of equipment. There's a whole lot of variety in these bad boys, each designed to suit different needs and purposes. Let's dive into the world of tail lifts!

First off, we have standard trolley lifts. They're used primarily for domestic and light commercial applications like small delivery vans or trucks. Slim and lightweight, they don't take up much space (or add much weight!) which is pretty handy when you've got a tight payload allowance. But remember, they ain't suitable for heavy-duty operations!

Next up is the cantilever lift – a real workhorse! Built tough as nails for those heavy loads (up to 3000 kilograms), this lift has flexible platforms that can extend or retract depending on load size. It's like a transformer, really! However, due to its complex design and heavy weight, it ain't be suitable for smaller vehicles.

Then we have column tail lifts – oh boy, these are something else! These bad boys are perfect for large vehicles handling bulky items. With their vertical lifting system, they enable easy loading at variable heights (handy!). But don't forget! They're not ideal for low-roofed premises because of their high lifting range.

Sliding tail lifts might also tickle your fancy. These fellas slide under the vehicle when not in use (sneaky!), providing unrestricted access to the cargo area. Great idea if you ask me! But there's always a downside - their lifting capacity ain't quite as high as other types.

Last but certainly not least are passenger tail lifts – specially designed for wheelchair-bound passengers. They make boarding buses or minibuses an absolute breeze (a real lifesaver!). Yet unfortunately, they aren't practical for transporting goods due to their specific design.

Well there you have it folks - different strokes for different folks as they say. Each type of tail lift has its own quirk, benefit, and limitation! Regardless of the job in hand, there's a tail lift out there to fit the bill. But remember – always take care when operating these machines, safety first!

So whether you're moving home or hauling heavy goods for your business, choosing the right tail lift can make all the difference. Get clued up and pick wisely!
