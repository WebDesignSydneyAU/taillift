Frequently Asked Questions

What is an Anteo tail lift?
An Anteo tail lift is a hydraulic or pneumatic lift system installed on vehicles such as trucks, vans, and lorries to help in loading and unloading goods. Its manufactured by Anteo, a renowned Italian company specializing in transport solutions.
How much does an Anteo tail lift cost?
The price of an Anteo tail lift can vary significantly based upon the model, capacity, and specifications. As such its best to contact the manufacturer or authorized dealer directly for the most accurate pricing information.
Does the price of an Anteo tail lift include installation costs?
Typically, the price of the tail lift itself doesnt include installation costs. Installation charges are usually separate and depend on factors like location and complexity of installation. However, some dealers may offer package deals that include both product and installation costs.
Where can I purchase an Anteo tail lift?
You can buy an Anteo tail lift either directly from the manufacturer or through their network of authorized dealers worldwide.
Are there any additional costs involved with owning an Anteo Tail Lift other than its initial purchase price?
Yes, owning a tail lift also involves maintenance and repair costs over its lifespan. Regular inspections are necessary to ensure optimal functionality and safety which could involve replacement parts if needed.
