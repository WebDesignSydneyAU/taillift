Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Dhollandia tail lift?
A Dhollandia tail lift is a hydraulic device installed at the rear of vehicles like trucks and vans, designed to assist in the loading and unloading of goods. It is manufactured by the company Dhollandia, one of Europes leading manufacturers of hydraulic tail lifts.
How does a Dhollandia tail lift operate?
A Dhollandia tail lift operates using a hydraulic system. When activated, it raises or lowers the platform onto which goods are loaded. This can be controlled by an operator using a remote control or switch panel usually located on the side of the vehicle.
What are some features that make Dhollandia tail lifts unique?
Some unique features of Dhollandia tail lifts include their exceptional build quality, reliability, and user-friendly operation. They come in various models with different lifting capacities to cater to specific needs. Additionally, they have safety measures such as non-slip surfaces and foot controls for safe operation.
